A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-xvader-tell-a-whole-story-in-32-characters-f86dd978) entry for the
**Tell a Whole Story in 32 Characters** bounty: one Wi-Fi network name of at most
32 characters that lands a joke and implies a whole story.

## The entry

> **She Took The Dog, I Kept WiFi** (29 characters)

A breakup settlement in one line: she got the dog, he got the Wi-Fi.
Two characters, one split, and the router as the consolation prize —
the whole story in the name a passer-by sees in their phone's network list.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the network name to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). It checks the gate first:
UTF-8 text of 1 to 32 characters, not only white space.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
